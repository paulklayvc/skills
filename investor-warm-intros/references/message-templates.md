# Message templates for warm-intro routes

Use these as structures, not copy-paste text. Every message must be rewritten around the specific person, company, and offer. Keep first-touch messages under ~120 words. Never attach a deck to a first touch in Routes 1, 3, or 4.

## Contents
1. Route 1 — First touch to a portfolio founder
2. Route 1 — Asking a portfolio founder for the intro (later)
3. Route 3 — Offering help to a portfolio company via the investor
4. Route 4 — Requesting advice from an investor-mentor
5. Route 4 — Follow-up session showing progress
6. Route 2 — Content ideas and tagging investors
7. Forwardable blurb (for any intro that's been agreed)

---

## 1. Route 1 — First touch to a portfolio founder

Structure: specific observation about their company → concrete thing you can give → no ask.

> Hi Maya — saw Loopwise just launched EU data residency. We went through the same SOC 2 + GDPR audit last quarter and built a vendor-questionnaire library that cut our security review time from weeks to days. Happy to send it over and walk your team through what auditors actually pushed on. No strings — just been through it recently.

Avoid: mentioning the fund, your round, or "would love to pick your brain".

## 2. Route 1 — Asking a portfolio founder for the intro (later)

Only after a real relationship exists. Casual, easy to say no to, and include a forwardable blurb.

> Maya — we're starting to talk to investors for our seed next month. Given how you've seen us work, would you be comfortable introducing me to Priya at Northfield? Totally fine if not. Short blurb below if it's helpful.

## 3. Route 3 — Offering help to a portfolio company via the investor

Structure: which portfolio company → the specific problem you noticed → exactly what you'll do, for free → why you're credible → no sell.

> Hi Priya — I noticed Tessel (in your portfolio) is hiring its first ML platform engineers in Lisbon. I ran ML infra hiring at [Company] and have a shortlist of ~15 strong platform engineers in Portugal plus a take-home we validated on 200+ candidates. I'd like to give Tessel both, free, and spend an hour with their CTO on the loop. Could you connect us? I'm not selling anything.

Checks before sending: Is the help specific? Is it something the company plausibly needs now? Could you deliver it at a high level this month? If any answer is no, don't send.

## 4. Route 4 — Requesting advice from an investor-mentor

Structure: why them specifically → one sharp problem → what you've already tried → the question. No pitch.

> Hi Daniel — your posts on usage-based pricing for dev tools are the clearest I've found. We sell observability to mid-size infra teams and are seeing expansion stall after month 3. We've tested seat caps and a free tier on alerts; neither moved it. Could I get 20 minutes to ask how you'd diagnose this? I'll come with the data.

## 5. Route 4 — Follow-up session showing progress

> Daniel — after our call we moved alerts to a usage meter as you suggested. Month-3 expansion went from 4% to 11% across 40 accounts. Two new problems came up (below). Would you be open to another 20 minutes in a couple of weeks?

The point is to show the loop: advice → action → result → next question.

## 6. Route 2 — Content ideas and tagging investors

Content that works for thesis-driven investors:
- "Top N builders / investors / open-source projects in [thesis]" lists
- Quote an investor's post or essay and add a genuine extension or respectful disagreement with data
- Original research: benchmarks, cost teardowns, market maps, surveys of practitioners
- Operator lessons with specifics ("what broke when we scaled X to Y")

Tag an investor only when the piece is genuinely useful or relevant to them (e.g. it builds on their essay or covers their thesis). One thoughtful tag beats ten.

## 7. Forwardable blurb

3–4 lines the introducer can forward untouched: what the company does, one traction proof point, why this investor specifically, and the ask.

> [Company] builds [one line]. [One traction proof: revenue, growth, notable customers, or technical milestone]. Raising a [$X] [stage] round; [Investor] came up because of [thesis fit / specific portfolio adjacency]. Would love 30 minutes.
