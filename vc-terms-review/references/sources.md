# Sources

Use these to verify a position, read the full model text behind a clause, or refresh market data. Prefer the newest edition of a report; most publish quarterly or annually under the same name.

## Model documents (baselines)

| Source | Use it for |
|---|---|
| [NVCA model legal documents](https://nvca.org/model-legal-documents/) | US priced rounds: charter, stock purchase, investors' rights, voting, ROFR and co-sale, management rights letter, indemnification, legal opinion. Footnotes explain each bracketed alternative |
| [NVCA certificate of incorporation (Oct 2025)](https://nvca.org/document/nvca-model-certificate-of-incorporation-updated-oct-2025/) | Preferences, DLEs, protective provisions, anti-dilution, redemption, pay-to-play |
| [NVCA stock purchase agreement (Oct 2025)](https://nvca.org/document/nvca-model-stock-purchase-agreement-updated-oct-2025/) | Reps and warranties, closing conditions, tranches |
| [NVCA investors' rights agreement (Oct 2025)](https://nvca.org/document/nvca-model-investors-rights-agreement-updated-oct-2025/) | Information, observer, confidentiality, pro rata, amendments |
| [NVCA voting agreement (June 2026)](https://nvca.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Voting-Agreement-Updated-June-2026.docx) | Board composition, removal, drag-along |
| [NVCA ROFR and co-sale agreement (April 2026)](https://nvca.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Right-of-First-Refusal-and-Co-Sale-Agreement-Updated-April-2026.docx) | Founder transfer restrictions |
| [NVCA management rights letter](https://nvca.org/document/nvca-2020-management-rights-letter-2/) | VCOC management rights |
| [NVCA indemnification agreement](https://nvca.org/document/nvca-2020-indemnification-agreement-2/) | Director indemnification, fund indemnitors |
| [NVCA model legal opinion](https://nvca.org/document/model-legal-opinion/) | Customary closing opinions |
| [Y Combinator documents](https://www.ycombinator.com/documents) | Post-money SAFE variants, pro rata side letter, SAFE user guide |
| [Series Seed](https://github.com/seriesseed/equity) | Lightweight seed preferred documents; a "clean seed terms" baseline |
| [Cooley GO documents](https://www.cooleygo.com/documents/) | Seed and Series A templates, term sheet generator |
| [Orrick Tech Studio](https://www.orrick.com/en/Tech-Studio) | US and European startup templates; Orrick's European deal data ("Deal Flow" report — search by name, the direct link moves) |
| [Founders Workbench](https://www.foundersworkbench.com/) | Founder-side document tools and checklists |
| [UK Private Capital (BVCA) model documents](https://www.ukprivatecapital.co.uk/policy/industry-guidance-standardised-documents/model-documents-for-early-stage-investments.html) | UK Series A summary of terms, subscription and shareholders' agreements, articles (free login required); [February 2025 changes](https://www.ukprivatecapital.co.uk/resource/bvca-model-documents-for-early-stage-investments-key-changes-to-february-2025-edition.html) |
| [HMRC venture capital schemes manual](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/venture-capital-schemes-manual/vcm12020) | SEIS and EIS share requirements and advance subscription agreements |
